First-time setup

Choose an administrator password (8 characters or more). It protects controls, settings, calibration and updates.

Controls

Open works in Manual or Maintenance only and keeps every protection active. Muting never clears a fault.

Calibration

Close the service isolation valve first. Capture uses the median of 21 settled readings. Any capture clears "commissioned" until you confirm it again in Settings.

Settings

Network and remote access

While asleep the unit does not measure, fill, or answer this page or the phone app. It stays awake during a fill, a fault, when the level is near the start threshold, and while this page is open. Press ACK to wake it, for example before a firmware update.

Firmware update

The unit disarms and closes the valve before accepting the file, then restarts disarmed.
